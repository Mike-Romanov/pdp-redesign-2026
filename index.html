The story

“Designed by Jean Paul Gaultier for Hermès and debuted in 2009, the JPG Kelly 42 is a modern take on a classic.”

A wider, shorter silhouette with an elongated strap, made to be worn over the shoulder as the name suggests. It is one of the few Kelly designs to come out of Jean Paul Gaultier's years as creative director of Hermès womenswear, from 2003 to 2010.
